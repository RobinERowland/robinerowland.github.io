I am a wildlife ecologist and PhD candidate at the University of the Sunshine Coast, with research focused on insectivorous bat ecology, urbanisation, roosting ecology, and wildlife conservation.

My work combines field ecology, physiological monitoring, spatial analysis, and large ecological datasets to investigate how bats respond to environmental change and human-modified landscapes.
