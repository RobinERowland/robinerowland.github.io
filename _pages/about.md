## Australian Bat Roost Database

An open-access database of verified roost associations for Australian bats, developed to improve access to information on bat roost ecology and support research, conservation, and management.

[**Explore the Australian Bat Roost Database**](https://australianbatroosts.shinyapps.io/AustralianBatRoostDatabase/){:target="_blank"}

## Bats in Buildings in South East Queensland

This project investigates where bats use buildings as roosts in South East Queensland and examines the impacts that living in anthropogenic structures has on roost residents.
